# FEAT-103: The R1 conclusion — a job site boss on the region wall

Step 6 of **Places and Missions**.

> **DESIGN OPEN — do not build** (owner, 2026-10-16): *"this idea is very underbaked and we should
> expect a lot of development before committing to anything."* What is ruled is below; everything
> else is a question for the owner.

## Ruled (owner, 2026-10-16)

- **Region 1 only, for now.** Whether R2/R3 get a job site, or a boss of a different form (a log
  drag, an expedition), is open.
- **It is the R1 boss: the mission that lets you leave region 1.** Simplest shape: a bridge build,
  with a foreman to match (MILESTONES 2026-10-12: the bridge site is the first boss; the log drag is
  a toy/scenario).
- **Gated by good deeds.** Completely unavailable until the player has the required good deeds, then
  always available. It is not in the job budget. The gate is the minimum: nothing about the mission is
  impossible for the stock truck, so the deeds are what make it a boss.

## The need it answers

**Region 1 needs a natural conclusion**: a challenge for the player and a way out of the region, in a
way that fits the story.

**And a new mission shape**: a job where the player is asked to bring many *different* items from
around the region, all filling one progress bar (or several) toward a larger reward. The bridge
meter (missions.md "the bridge site") is the first instance.

## Open — the owner's

1. **What does failing the boss cost?** A roguelike boss would kill the run — but how that fits the
   story is unknown. The alternative is opportunity cost: fail and you start over, which spends days,
   and the costs overwhelm you.
2. **Bridge, or something else for R1?** The bridge's natural suppliers are the lumber yard and the
   quarry, which are R2 and R3 (FEAT-91). Either the yards are rearranged to feed the bridge, or the
   bridge moves later and the R1 boss becomes something else, e.g. **a camp or expedition searching
   for the road out of town**.
3. **How many good deeds** open it.
4. **Placement**: on the R1 wall (SM-INV-13: the gate is a place), within ~300 m of it, deterministic.

## Acceptance
