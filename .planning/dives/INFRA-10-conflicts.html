story (5)

DEC-024The clock stands still while the parking brake is set
No game time passes while the parking brake is latched, including after leaving a camp or shop until it is released; entering a menu that needs the truck parked sets it, and only throttle (or shifting into gear on a manual) releases it.
DEC-037Spirits are beta
The spirit cast and pacts are beta, not the alpha; spirits.md carries flagged conflicts and is deferred.
DEC-040The firing plays as an in-world camera move with story cards
For now the firing is an in-world camera move with Story cards over the live world, not a graphic-novel sequence.
DEC-042An alpha run ends by winning, dying or giving up, then shows a summary
An alpha run ends three ways — the expedition camp is beaten, the player dies, or the player gives up from the pause menu — and each ends on a run summary.
DEC-051A job is player state, not world state
Never gate pumps, shops or camp on a mission: holding a job changes what the player is doing, not what the world offers.

economy (6)

DEC-020Papers jobs rank on time against a deadline, and it is called a deadline
A papers job ranks on time against a deadline, for any number of stops; the player-facing word is "deadline", never "bell".
DEC-022A bonus item is a choice the player can take or leave
A mission's item reward is optional: the player may take the item or its cash value, and an item can be earned at any rank but D. Missions that paid $0 plus an item are retired.
DEC-023A job's deadline is par times its slack, on the world clock
A job's deadline is par × (1 + slack) measured on world time, and the player may camp with a job in hand; a job still held past its deadline expires and pays nothing.
DEC-026Rising costs are what push a run along; the storm is not planned
Prices scale with the clock only, and that cost escalation is what pushes a run forward. The spreading "storm" front is not built and not planned.
DEC-032Each station's fuel price varies by ±5 %
Fuel is distance's cost; every station's price carries a factor of ±5 %, seeded by the world, the station and the day.
DEC-049The alpha has one haul yard: the region-1 splitting yard
The alpha's haul givers are the region-1 splitting yard only; the sawmill and quarry are beta. Haul yards post haul only.

world (6)

DEC-048Missions and POIs sit mid-edge, never on nodes
Mission points and POIs sit mid-edge along roads, never on junction nodes.
DEC-053The regions are "the ladder"
The three regions are ring sections stacked north to south, and they are called "the ladder", never "rings".
DEC-054A split network never refuses a seed; a condemned road does
A road network split into pieces no longer refuses a seed; a road that can't be given a drivable profile (a condemned edge) still refuses it at story entry, failing safe and saying so.
DEC-055Story mode only prunes or restores roads, never builds them
The story layer may trim roads (at a region wall) and restore them; it never adds a road the world did not generate.
DEC-057Every world is generated cold; no worldgen data in saves
Every world is generated from scratch at load; a save carries the seed and knobs, never worldgen data.
DEC-061Skips are the beta Highway/Shortcut pair
Fast routes past the network (the Highway/Shortcut pair) are beta, and their tickets are minted at entry.
