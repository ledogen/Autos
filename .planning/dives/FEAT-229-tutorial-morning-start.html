FEAT-229: the tutorial starts the run in the morning

Today the firing is at 19:30 and the tutorial lasts exactly 24 in-game hours, so free will opens at 19:30. A new player has to sleep at once or drive all night. You want the run to open on a fresh morning, with every tutorial lesson kept. This page shows the tutorial as it is built today, then four ways to re-order it, then the clock question on its own. The beat order, the firing and the clock are yours to pick. Seeds are out of scope here: as you said, the beats come first, and a seed gets picked to fit them afterwards. Distances below use the current tutorial seed (822480141) only to give the times a realistic scale.

0. Four facts every option leans on

  • The day is 24 real minutes (DAY_PARAMS.dayLengthSec = 1440, ratified in run-shape.md). So one in-game hour is one real minute.
  • A new player averages roughly 50 km/h including corners and stops. That is about 1.2 real minutes per km. With the clock running, each km of road also costs about 1.2 in-game hours. This is the number that makes a running clock hard to control in a tutorial: a slow driver loses hours.
  • The sky (src/sky.js): sunrise 05:33, sunset 20:18, full dark from 21:18 to 04:33.
  • Energy (src/day.js): a full night buys 16 h awake. Woken at 07:00, the driver is sleepy at 19:00 (steering slows to ×0.75), tired at 21:00, and exhausted at 23:00 (steering ×0.5).

3. The clock: how each option gets to a brand new day

The seam

A seam is the moment the clock is allowed to jump without the player feeling cheated. Sleep at mom's is the natural one: the player chooses it, the timer shows the hours, and waking at 07:00 is what a day's start looks like everywhere else in the game (16 h awake + 8 h asleep closes the day, run-shape.md). A uses it in the middle, as today. B and C use it at the end. D has none, so free will starts on the firing day. Other jumps that already exist and read as honest: the tow's wait (3 h + 0.5 h per km), the shop's labour hours, and a card. The paycheck's jump today is a card too, but a 6 h jump in daylight is the part that feels like a hack.

The clock inside the tutorial

Three ways to run it:

  • Held, with stamps (recommended): the clock does not move on the road, as today's evening. Each stop sets a written time (a "stamp"): arriving at Larry's sets 12:30, the paycheck 20:00, and so on. Every player sees the same sky at the same beat, whatever their speed or seed. The cost: the sun jumps at each stop, under its card.
  • Running, as free will: the sky moves smoothly, but at 1.2 in-game hours per km the order only works at about 50 km/h (option B's worked example shows the spread).
  • Running, with floors: the clock runs, and a stop lifts it to at least its time if the player is early (C's paycheck to 20:00, B's shop to 21:15). It guarantees the dark lessons. A slow player still overshoots, and a fast one sees a bigger jump.

Energy follows the clock. Fired at 07:00 with a full night behind them, a player in B or C is sleepy at 19:00 and tired at 21:00, so the night drive home steers slower (×0.75, then lower). That could be a free lesson in tiredness just before mom's bed, or it could be held at full for the whole tutorial, as today's evening holds it.

Where a skip lands

DEC-152 says a skip lands where and when the intro ends; DEC-159 says played and skipped runs enter free will on the same day number. With a morning-start tutorial that lasts a whole day (A, B, C), both cannot hold. One fact softens it: every day up to day 1 costs the same (dayTier clamps day −1, 0 and 1 to the first price tier), so a skipper who starts a day earlier gets one extra day at the floor price. A played run is never charged more.

  • The next morning, outside mom's: a "the next morning" card, the manager's check already handed over. Keeps DEC-159; supersedes DEC-152's skip clause.
  • The next morning, outside the joint: the same, at the joint.
  • The firing morning, outside the joint: the skipper's run starts at about 07:05 on the firing day. Keeps DEC-152's skip clause; supersedes DEC-159.

In option D the question disappears: the skip lands at the joint at the firing time, and both runs share day 0.

4. The firing: time and cause

Options B, C and D move the firing to the morning. Your idea: the shift starts at 06:30, the player arrives at 07:00, and being late is why they are fired. 07:00 is an hour and a half after sunrise: low sun from the east, long shadows across the joint's lot. Today's line ("The grill's been cold twenty minutes and there's a line out the door") already reads as someone who did not show up, so it nearly works as is. The camera orbit and the cards do not change.

5. Larry's call

DEC-155: Larry calls after the paycheck, "so right after you get free will". In A and D the paycheck is still the tutorial's last beat, so nothing changes. In B and C the paycheck is no longer the end. His call can come on waking at mom's, which keeps the record's intent (the call opens free will). Or it can stay at the paycheck, mid-tutorial, which supersedes it. In both B and C you also meet him in person for the pack, so his four cards could be spoken there.
