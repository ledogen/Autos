Tutorial flow: the issues you asked for

On the skip flow you wrote "lemme know if there are issues with it in the next round". These are the issues, checked against the code. Each has a recommendation; pick it or change it. They are on one page because they lean on each other: which world the tutorial plays in decides how many loads there are, and the tutorial's length decides which day free will starts on.

Already built from your answers, because nothing below blocks it (Lane A, landing 1): no setting and no "always skip"; the prompt reads "hold M to skip tutorial"; the extra confirmation on the first playthrough ever and when you chose the tutorial; the traps (no fuel burn and no damage on the evening, off the road puts you back on it, mom makes you fill until the pocket change is gone, the shop's tire is on mom); pocket change until the paycheck at the end.

1. One world at a time, so the intro cannot hide a load

The game holds one world: one road network, one terrain, one physics world. Building another means replacing that one, behind the loading screen. The intro is staged in the world (the camera circles the real burger joint), so the world it plays in has to be built before it starts. "Load the story seed during the intro" is not possible; the load before the intro is the one you already see after "start".

Any later run (intro on its random seed)1 load2 loads: the tutorial seed is built at the card

Recommendation: accept the second load. Only a returning player who chooses to replay the tutorial sees it.

2. Which world a played tutorial continues in

The tutorial ends at the tutorial seed's burger joint and flows straight into free will. On the first playthrough that is fine: the run's world is the tutorial seed. On a later run the intro played on a random world, and the tutorial on a different one. When it ends, free will has to be in one of them.

  • The tutorial seed (recommended): a run that plays the tutorial is played on the tutorial seed, the same as the first playthrough. No load at the end, so the end stays seamless.
  • Back to the random world: a third load at the tutorial's end, then outside that world's burger joint.

3. The day counter

Costs ride the day (DEC-025): parts, repairs, the tow and fuel are × 1.065(day − 1), with day 0 counted as day 1. The firing is at 19:30 on day 0. A skip enters free will at 19:30 on day 0, so its first working morning is day 1 (1.00×). A played tutorial enters free will 24 h later, at 19:30 on day 1, so its first working morning is day 2 (1.065×). The tutorial player pays 6.5 % more for everything for the whole run, and is one day further into the escalation.

Recommendation: both enter free will on the same day number. The tutorial's 24 hours are counted as the day before (it starts at 19:30 on the eve of day 0).

4. "Exactly 24 h", and the ~18 h without a burger job

The evening holds the clock, the tow and the shop move it to about midnight, and sleep sets 07:00. The morning runs on the real clock, so the player reaches the burger joint at whatever hour they get there Nothing makes that 19:30 on its own.

Recommendation: the paycheck card sets the clock to 19:30, as sleep sets 07:00 (the manager made you wait all day). The alternative is a card that sends you away until 19:30, which leaves the player an empty day inside the tutorial.

The firing is at 19:30, not 18:00 (FEAT-180 moved it for the dusk light). 18 h from 19:30 is 13:30 the next day, which is not "early next day". 07:00 (when the day starts) is 11.5 h.

5. Does the casserole stay?

Two of your answers read differently. First-job: "tutorial contains the casserole mission". Skip: "go back to pick up your last paycheck from the burger joint, which replaces the casserole mission". Your story answer has Larry remind you of the camp when you arrive at his house, which only happens if the casserole stays.

Recommendation: keep it. The morning is breakfast, mom's casserole to Larry's (unpaid, his one reminder of the camp), then back to the burger joint for the paycheck as the last act.

6. Who tells a skipper what the run is for

Today a skip still hears Larry's welcome and the run intro (the goal, ten good deeds, the expedition camp and its direction, the pay rule). That is the one card that says what the run is for: the map shows the camp, but nothing says why. A skip outside the burger joint meets neither mom nor Larry.

Recommendation: after the boss's line, Larry calls (like mom's call today) and his run intro plays. The words are yours.

7. The menu's "tutorial" button and "the tutorial never saves"

On 11-08 the tutorial became a main-menu button, and a tutorial run never touches the career save. Once every new game offers the tutorial at the card, and the tutorial flows into free will as the start of the run, the button duplicates the card and the no-save rule would lose the run.

Recommendation: retire both when the card lands. Until then the button stays, and counts as "chose the tutorial" for the confirmation.
