# DEC-117: Ticket classes are feature, bug, perf, quality, infra and asset

**Ruling.** Every ticket is one of feature (FEAT), bug (BUG), perf (PERF), quality (QUAL), infra (INFRA) or asset (ASSET); there are no CONCERN- or IDEA- ids.

**Why.** The dashboard filters by these classes.

**Replaces.** "use CONCERN-/IDEA- for the rest" (memory feedback_scribe_lane (2026-06-10)).
