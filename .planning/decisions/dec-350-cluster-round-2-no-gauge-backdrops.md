# DEC-350: Cluster round 2 — frame wraps the speedometer, no gauge backdrops, key at the bottom right

**Ruling.** Owner, card feat235-cluster-look, 2026-11-16: "This is better than what's on main, so land it. I'd like to
see the frame wrap around the speedometer and go straight down to reduce the overall width. Feel free to move the key
wherever, but it Should generally be to the bottom right of the speedometer. I think the main difference between our
design and the reference is we have a light gray bezel then we have a dark gray pod outline then we put the gauges as
darker circles directly on the pod outline the real reference just has a bezel and then the gauges are directly on the
pods with no intermediate gray. I think what we probably want is to get rid of the intermediate gray and go straight to
the dark pod background behind the gauges. So basically delete the circular gauge backdrops and merge with the sculpted
dark gray pod backgrounds. That'll mean we have all new negative space to play with so we get to redistribute the
warning lights and maybe some of the text"

**Why.** The owner's look at round 1 against the real Ranger cluster: the reference has a bezel and gauges printed
straight on the pod face, with no second grey between them.

**Replaces.** Nothing; round 1 (DEC-331) is landed and this is the next round.
