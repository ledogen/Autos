# DEC-449: Food shows how long until it spoils, so the player knows how long they can sleep

**Ruling.** "Food spoiling should have a timer so you know how long you can sleep and not lose it."

**Why.** Sleep jumps the clock; without a number the player loses food to a sleep they could have shortened.

**Replaces.** Nothing. It adds to DEC-338's freshness bar (the bar stays).
