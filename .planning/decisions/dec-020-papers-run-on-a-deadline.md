# DEC-020: Papers jobs rank on time against a deadline, and it is called a deadline

**Ruling.** A papers job ranks on time against a deadline, for any number of stops; the player-facing word is "deadline", never "bell".

**Why.** "How close am I" is the question the readout answers; one word for one idea across every card.

**Replaces.** Nothing.
