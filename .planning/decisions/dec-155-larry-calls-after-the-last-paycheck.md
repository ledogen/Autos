# DEC-155: Larry calls after the last paycheck

**Ruling.** On a skip, "Larry calls after the boss's line". In the tutorial, "larry calls you after you pick up your last paycheck (so right after you get free will). he offers you a side gig delivering papers. he reminds you of the expedition camp and good deeds."

**Why.** A skip meets neither mom nor Larry; the call is what tells every player what the run is for.

**Replaces.** Larry's welcome at his house after the casserole (FEAT-170) as the run intro's carrier.
