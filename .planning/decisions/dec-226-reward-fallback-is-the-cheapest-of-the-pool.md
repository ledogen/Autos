# DEC-226: When no item fits the price window, the job offers the cheapest of its pool

**Ruling.** "fine as built (cheapest of the pool as last resort)". And on a stock engine offered by a long job: "im cool with that for a 10km job!"

**Why.** A job with an item pool always shows an item; the cap (never more than the job paid, DEC-176) already limits the cost. How players reach items far above the window (other engines, turbo, supercharger) is a separate, open call.

**Replaces.** Nothing; extends DEC-204.

**Source.** Card feat199-never, 2026-11-15. Owner's words.
