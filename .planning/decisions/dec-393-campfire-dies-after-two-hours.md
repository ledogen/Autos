# DEC-393: The campfire is dead after a sleep of more than two hours

**Ruling.** "After sleeping at the camp site for any amount of time over two hours you wake up and the campfire is dead. No more flames and smoke particle effects rising from the ashes. Let's also remake a campfire model that doesn't have the three logs, just ashes at the bottom."

**Why.** A fire left alone all night does not burn until morning.

**Replaces.** The comment in `_renderCampUI` that "the fire is lit all camp". No record governed it.

**Source.** Owner inbox 20261117103006-j5xw, 2026-11-17. Owner's words. Relighting and cooking at a dead fire are NOT ruled (FEAT-264 open questions).
