# DEC-346: The tutorial runs errands first; the drive to the camping area is on a waypoint, and Katie teaches the camp there

**Ruling.** DEC-304's order, unchanged: "1: joint, errands first" (fired at the joint; to mom's; Larry's old pack to
Larry's; the station for a snack and the fill-up; a camping area to eat it; the flat, the tow and the shop on the way
home; sleep at mom's; next morning back to the joint for the last paycheck; free will there). The drive back to the joint
for the check is "the last portion on rails before you have free will". DEC-345 changes how the camping area is reached
and taught: "Since Katie's already given you the instructions, once you fill up with gas and mom sends you on your way,
you already have a waypoint to the nearest camping area. The guiding text can read "drive out to the old McGil ranch".
When you arrive at the waypoint, Katie calls you ... And she can guide you through the camping menu and the
Terrain-based vibe enhancers." As built: the tank filled, mom's send-off card (`tut-snackcamp`); the GPS line already
runs to the door of the camping area nearest the station, the map shows a Waypoint pin on that door (the tutorial's, never
saved, gone on arrival), and the HUD reads "drive out to the old McGill ranch · <km>". Arrived (inside a camp zone, or
within the waypoint's 28 m of the door), Katie's call (`tut-ranch`: the owner's line, then how to look for a site); looking,
the vibe bar's four terrain segments (`tut-vibe`: flat, view, shade, water); camp made, the snack (`tut-campmade`).

**Why.** The picnic (DEC-345) gives the player the destination before the drive, so the drive is guided; the camp lesson
DEC-304 wanted ("what buttons to press ... the flow of opening the camping cart") moves into Katie's voice at the site.

**Replaces.** DEC-304's "I want the player to drive from the gas station to the camping area themselves" (as built the
line already led there; the waypoint and the guiding text are new) and mom's camp card. The order stands.
