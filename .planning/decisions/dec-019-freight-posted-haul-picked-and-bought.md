# DEC-019: Freight is posted; haul is picked and bought

**Ruling.** Freight is posted, not picked: the load and its mass are the offer's, delivery is binary, and the bonus is earned on delivery. Haul is picked: the player chooses the count from the giver's limited stock and buys each piece at half the job's base pay per piece.

**Why.** "Somebody needs something shipped so they post a mission about it"; a priced haul piece makes the spare pieces a real choice, kept cheap because "I don't want them to gamble $20 on a $30 mission".

**Replaces.** "Load what you dare" and the freight picker (2026-10-21 §2, §5), freight units (FEAT-140, cancelled); the rolled 10–15 bundle count (2026-10-05). Settled by FEAT-103 (alpha): the expedition camp's firewood line is 20 bundles, free — a requisition, not a haul job.
