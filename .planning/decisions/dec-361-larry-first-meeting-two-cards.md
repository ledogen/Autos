# DEC-361: Larry's first meeting is two cards: the greasebeard line, then the work

**Ruling.** Owner, card tutorial-test-drives, 2026-11-17, verbatim: "Larry should say the grease beard line from the intro On the first day when you drive to his house. In general, six cards for the player to click through is way too long in the beginning of the run so it should just be something brief from Larry like It's too bad you got fired. I have some work if you need it. Remember to check out the expedition camp. Like two cards at most." As built: LARRY_PACK is two cards, the first carrying the greasebeard line and "It's too bad you got fired", the second the work, the pack to keep, and the expedition camp (the pack clause is an agent's placeholder wording).

**Why.** Four cards (and the six before the first drive) were too long at the run's start.

**Replaces.** DEC-322's four-card LARRY_PACK (the paper-route offer card and the pack hand-over, as four). DEC-155's skip half and DEC-326's call stand.
