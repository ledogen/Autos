# DEC-181: On an Impatient calm job the clock decides the tip only

**Ruling.** "the tip only; the deed stays on the type's own axis". The bar on an Impatient fragile / haul / freight job shows the full-tip line alone; the deed stays on condition / count / kg and no item gate rides the bar.

**Why.** DEC-018 stands: the tip is speed and rank is the type's own axis. Time does not become a second grader.

**Replaces.** Nothing standing (the alternative, a deed dropping a rung on a missed time line, was declined).
