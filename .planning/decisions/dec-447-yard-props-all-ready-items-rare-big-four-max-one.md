# DEC-447: Yard props: every ready item joins; the fountain, sculptures, vase and hi-lift jack are much rarer and max one per yard; the dead campfire is killed

**Ruling.** "And all the ready ones make the garden fountain, the sculpture, the vase, and the high lift much rarer than the other items. For the max of one per yard. Kill the dead campfire." (card choice: "I'll list which in the note box")

**Why.** The yard needs more props, but the big pieces should read as special: one per yard at most, and seldom.

**Replaces.** Nothing replaces it. The agent's reading of "all the ready ones" and of "kill the dead campfire" (out of the yard pool) is in FEAT-290's Open questions; the owner's words above are the ruling. Related: DEC-394 (only the fire pit is solid; the rest are knockable bodies), DEC-405.

**Source.** Owner, card feat290-yard-props-pick, answered 2026-11-19. Owner's words.
