# DEC-289: Under the parking brake the world clock stops, the job's time does not

**Ruling.** "I don't like the way pulling the handbrake during a mission pauses the mission timer. I think mission and rank calculation and deadline should all continue to scroll But world clock should be stopped when the truck is Parked with the Handbrake."

**Why.** A parked handbrake was a free pause on the job: the rank bar and the deadline stood still while the player sat.

**Replaces.** DEC-024's world-clock rule carries over unchanged: no world time passes while the parking brake is latched (energy, hunger, the sky and every offer's expiry stand still), menus that need the truck parked set it, and only throttle (or a gear on the manual, or reverse on the automatic, DEC-255) releases it. Only the job timer changes: a job or paper round in hand keeps spending its time while parked, at the driving rate (its elapsed, rank and tip, its deadline and the board's slot lock). DEC-023's "measured on world time" now means world time plus parked time.

**Source.** Owner inbox, 2026-11-15 (FEAT-228). Owner's words.
