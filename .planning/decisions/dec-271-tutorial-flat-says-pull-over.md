# DEC-271: After the tutorial's flat the guidance line reads "Pull over and check your tire"

**Ruling.** "After the tire goes out in the tutorial, the top guidance text should change to "Pull over and check your tire" Instead of still reading Park there to sleep." From the flat until the tire is fixed (at the shop), the top line is that order alone, in the HUD's lower case.

**Why.** The old line kept pointing at mom's, so a player could miss the flat and drive on.

**Replaces.** The evening line ("… to Mom's · park there to sleep") while the flat is on.

**Source.** Owner, owed card feat217-try, 2026-11-15. Owner's words; the lower case (the HUD's other lines) is the agent's.
