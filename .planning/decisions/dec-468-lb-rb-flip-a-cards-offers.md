# DEC-468: LB and RB flip a card's offers when it has more than one

**Ruling.** Owner, 2026-11-20, told that the POI offer card shows an RB keycap on its next-offer button that no button answered off the map: "fix the stale keycap. if a card has multiple offers, lb/rb should flip them. I recall this being how it worked, but I guess I probably just backed out of the menu before driving off instead of hitting RB."

Orchestrator's reading, flagged: it holds in both pad layouts. On a card with more than one offer, LB is the previous offer and RB the next, so in Professor RB is not the handbrake on that card (B still backs out). On a card with one offer the keycaps do not show and RB keeps its DEC-467 role (Professor: the handbrake; Ventura Highway: nothing).

**Why.** The card drew a key it did not answer. The owner expected the shoulders to flip offers.

**Replaces.** Nothing. It adds to DEC-467, which already gave Ventura Highway RB/LB on cards with tabs or faces.

**Source.** Owner, chat with the orchestrator, 2026-11-20.
