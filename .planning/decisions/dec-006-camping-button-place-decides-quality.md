# DEC-006: Camping is a button; the place decides how well you sleep

**Ruling.** You commit to sleep with an explicit action and the game previews the site's quality where you stand. You can always sleep in the truck, at a flat 1 h of energy per hour slept; a campsite always rests better.

**Why.** The last leg of the day is finding good ground, not the press itself; the camp gate decides how well you sleep, not whether.

**Replaces.** "Camping is a place, not a button" (reversed 2026-07-19); "Some regions are campable and some are not, so the button is gated" as a whether-gate (amended 2026-10-19).
