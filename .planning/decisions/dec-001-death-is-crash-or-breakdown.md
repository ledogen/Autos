# DEC-001: Death is a fatal crash or an unrecoverable breakdown, nothing else

**Ruling.** Death is crash or breakdown only; dozing is not a fail state. A story difficulty setting may grant lives (a lost life resets the truck to the road, loses the cargo and floors condition at 25 %), and respawn exists only as a development and free-roam tool.

**Why.** The only way to die must be the physics: the organizing problem ("why not drive slow forever?") is answered by the economy, never by a new fail state. Giving up from the pause menu is the player's choice, not a fail state, so the invariant holds.

**Replaces.** Nothing in its core. The lives clause (2026-10-12) and the give-up ending (2026-10-23, renamed from "throw in the towel" 2026-10-31) were added without changing what death is.
