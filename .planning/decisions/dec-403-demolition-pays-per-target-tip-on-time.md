# DEC-403: Demolition pays per target as each one falls; the tip is on time only

**Ruling.** "demolition - lets divide the base pay by the number of targets and pay per target. then not only do you get dopamine on hit, you still get paid even if the timer expires. tip on time only"

**Why.** His words: a payment on every hit feels good, and a job that runs out of time still pays for what was smashed. FEAT-267 as first built followed the paper round exactly: the job settled only with every target down and an expired job paid nothing.

**Replaces.** FEAT-267's all-or-nothing settle for demolition. It is an owner-made exception, for demolition only, to an expired job paying nothing (DEC-004); DEC-399 (demolition is a hurried job on the papers' deadline and planner) stands.

**Source.** Chat with the orchestrator, 2026-11-17, after the FEAT-267 report. Owner's words.
