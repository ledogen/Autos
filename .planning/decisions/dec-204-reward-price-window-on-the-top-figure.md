# DEC-204: Reward price window on the top figure

**Ruling.** "top figure, 0.7× to 1.3×": the item a job offers is priced from 0.7x to 1.3x of the card's top figure (base pay with the full tip), falling back to cheaper items when none fit. The owner adds: "lemme know which items will never be offered as a reward due to this rule. my guess is the engines, lsds and other top items will likely never be offered as a reward".

**Why.** The price window DEC-176 asked for needed a pay basis and a lower bound.

**Replaces.** Nothing; completes DEC-176 ("about the job's pay, up to 30 % over").

**Source.** Card feat199-window, 2026-11-14. Owner's words.
