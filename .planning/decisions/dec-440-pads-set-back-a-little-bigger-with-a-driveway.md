# DEC-440: POI pads: set back 2 to 3 metres, a little bigger, with a real driveway

**Ruling.** "I think we could rework pads in general to move the pad back a little bit, increase its size a little bit, and then actually build a driveway. I'm only talking pushing the pad back maybe two, three meters. I think when you get away from the road a little bit, you can take a lot more freedom with the pad making Without doing too much earthwork and then connecting it with a driveway or something It would also be a little more fun to drive on occasion. Some POIs have parking lots, so you have to think of how the driveway should logically attach to the parking lot so that you can access all the spaces."

**Why.** A pad butted against the road is hard to drive into and spoils the terrain; a short setback gives room for a driveway.

**Replaces.** Replaces today's pad-on-the-shoulder placement. No standing record found that fixes the setback.
