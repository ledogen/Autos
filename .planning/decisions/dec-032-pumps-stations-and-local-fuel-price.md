# DEC-032: Each station's fuel price varies by ±5 %

**Ruling.** Fuel is distance's cost; every station's price carries a factor of ±5 %, seeded by the world, the station and the day.

**Why.** Small, deterministic variation gives the player a reason to notice which pump they stop at without breaking SM-INV-12.

**Replaces.** One flat price at every pump.

**Source.** FEAT-209 (owner inbox 2026-11-13; built, open questions stand); DESIGN.md § Fuel and gas stations. Agent wording, owner approved 2026-11-13.
