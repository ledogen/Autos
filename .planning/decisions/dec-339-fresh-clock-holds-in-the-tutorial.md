# DEC-339: The fresh clock holds for the whole tutorial

**Ruling.** (Agent's wording, awaiting the owner.) Food does not age during the tutorial. The fresh clock starts when
free will does.

**Why.** The tutorial runs 24 h (DEC-299) and mom's three packed lunches are fresh for 24 h (items.md, DEC-338). With
the fresh clock running they spoil at the moment of the next morning's breakfast, whose card asks the player to eat
one (FEAT-184 stage 5). Main's full smoke caught it on 2026-11-16 ("no packed lunch for breakfast") while the owner was
asleep. The tutorial already wears nothing (DEC-151); this is the same rule for food, and the smallest change.

**Replaces.** Nothing. Alternatives for the owner: mom hands a fresh lunch at breakfast; or the starting lunches get a
longer fresh time.

**Source.** Orchestrator, 2026-11-16 night; smoke log wip/orch/smoke-1116-fresh.log.
