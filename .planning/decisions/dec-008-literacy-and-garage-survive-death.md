# DEC-008: Only literacy and the garage survive death

**Ruling.** What survives death is player literacy and the garage (unlocked starting vehicles and story keys) — not parts, not money, not the car, not the world and not XP. A won run's truck configuration may be saved for free roam only (FEAT-205), which carries nothing into a story run.

**Why.** A returning player isn't stronger, they're fluent; persistent world state or progress would be a power floor.

**Replaces.** "World state (permanent unlocks, generator parameter states) persists" (struck 2026-07-29). Settled by FEAT-205 (alpha): a winning truck kept for free roam.
