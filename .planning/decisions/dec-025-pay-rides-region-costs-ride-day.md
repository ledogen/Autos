# DEC-025: Pay rides the region; costs ride the day; no reputation in the alpha

**Ruling.** What a mission pays steps with the region, and the day tier scales only what things cost. There is no reputation track in the alpha (Larry is the one existing exception).

**Why.** Cost escalation over the run is the push; the region step is the raise.

**Replaces.** The day tier on pay (left pay 2026-10-14). Settled by FEAT-91 (alpha): DESIGN.md's "capped regional reputation" on pay is parked.

**Source.** DESIGN.md performance-model table ("what things cost", 2026-10-14); FEAT-91 ruling 1 ("No reputation for now"). Owner's words.
