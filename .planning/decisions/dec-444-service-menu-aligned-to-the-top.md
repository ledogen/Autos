# DEC-444: The service menu is aligned to the top, not centred on the right

**Ruling.** "Instead of having the service menu centered on the right, can we align it to the top? Then when sections open, it won't change where the entry is vertically on the screen. It's a lot to track with your eyes."

**Why.** A centred menu moves its entries when a section opens; a top-aligned one keeps them where the eye is.

**Replaces.** Replaces the vertical centring of the service menu. DEC-165, DEC-166 and DEC-246 (look, row pitch, pad highlight) stand.
