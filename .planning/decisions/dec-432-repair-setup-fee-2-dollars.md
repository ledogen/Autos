# DEC-432: Repair setup fee 2 dollars

**Ruling.** "just drop the setup fee to $2"

**Why.** The owner first asked to move component repair costs and tire swap costs by 25 %, then chose this instead: the shop's minimum charge that starts every repair goes from $5 to $2. The repair table's rates, the tire labor (bead $2, hub $1), the flat patch and the oil change do not move.

**Replaces.** The $5 placeholder of 2026-10-23 on `REPAIR_SETUP.price` (a [DEFAULT], no record). The half hour of access time stays. A bolt-on's fitting charge is this fee times the socket's multiplier, so it drops with it. Not superseded: DEC-419 (spring and damper rate $4).

**Source.** Owner, in chat, 2026-11-19. Owner's words.
