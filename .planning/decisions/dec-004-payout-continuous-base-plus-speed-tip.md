# DEC-004: Completion pays half the max; speed earns the other half

**Ruling.** "You get 50% of the possible max value for a mission from completion alone; going quickly and getting S rank gives you the other 50%, C rank 25%" — continuous and linear in the par ratio, floored at zero, and no pay term ever reads the letter. A job still in hand past its deadline expires and pays nothing.

**Why.** Safe driving isn't punished, it just doesn't pay; the tip keeps its value through the run ("we want to keep some pressure on to drive fast").

**Replaces.** The 2026-08-01 anchors (+20 % over par pays ~nothing) and the 2026-08-16 "break-even at B/C, par loses money" table; "bare completion pays ~nothing" struck 2026-09-30.
