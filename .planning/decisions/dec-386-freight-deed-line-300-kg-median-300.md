# DEC-386: Freight's deed line is an even 300 kg, and the median freight post should weigh 300 kg

**Ruling.** "raise the threshold to an even 300kg. We just need to add more heavy cargo Variants. Possibly trim some lighter variants. The median freight job should be 300kg, so half the options are 1 deed and really heavy and half the offers are half a deed and can be relatively easy for steady earners. pay should also scale with weight." Loads are added or trimmed only after the owner approves the two lists on the owed card feat69-freight-lists.

**Why.** Half the freight posts then earn a whole deed; the rest are the easy half-deed jobs for steady earners.

**Replaces.** Freight B 285 kg (R-18, 2026-10-24); the dive's "lower B to 100 kg" proposal.
