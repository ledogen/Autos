# DEC-406: Keep every freight job under 300 kg; add heavy cargo until half the offered jobs are 300 kg or more

**Ruling.** "Let's keep all of our under 300 kilogram jobs. Then picking from your list from least effort first, add just enough cargo to get the median to 300 kilograms. I think it's important to keep a lot of the lighter weight jobs because if you're only getting half a deed you might want the lightest job possible so it ends up that the ones at the bottom of the scale are kind of good in their own way. We could limit the propane pack to from residences, general store, and gas station, and then to residences and gas station, and maybe that would thin the pool a little bit. Or we could artificially limit the frequency. The goal is 300 plus kilogram jobs are as frequent as under 300 kilogram jobs."

**Why.** Light jobs stay as the easy half-deed jobs; the heavy half is built by adding cargo, not by killing light cargo.

**Replaces.** Nothing. It carries out DEC-386's "possibly trim some lighter variants" as "keep them all"; the 300 kg line and the median goal of DEC-386 stand.
