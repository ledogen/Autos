# DEC-296: The bay light is 20 % brighter; its spill past the walls is accepted for now

**Ruling.** "Light placement is good. It's honestly still pretty dark and dingy in the service center at night. So make
the light 20% brighter. It's okay that it spills out of the walls for now because we'll be adding shadows to this light."

**Why.** The shop read dark and dingy at night with the light at 40 cd.

**Replaces.** The bay light's 40 cd (DEC-228): now 48 cd, same place ([-1.8, 2.0, 0] model frame) and same 9 m range.
The spill through the walls and doors is accepted for now. Shadows on this light are not ruled in yet: DEC-227 (no
real-time light shadows) still stands until a record supersedes it.

**Source.** Owner, 2026-11-15, card `bay-light-look`, relayed by the orchestrator. Owner's words.
