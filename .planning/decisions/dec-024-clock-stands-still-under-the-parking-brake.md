# DEC-024: The clock stands still while the parking brake is set

**Ruling.** No game time passes while the parking brake is latched, including after leaving a camp or shop until it is released; entering a menu that needs the truck parked sets it, and only throttle (or shifting into gear on a manual) releases it.

**Why.** Parked time is the player reading the game, not playing it; the brake lamp is the visible sign the clock is held.

**Replaces.** Nothing.

**Source.** FEAT-194 source (owner, 2026-11-11 inbox, three items) and part 3 (owner, 2026-11-13). Agent wording.
