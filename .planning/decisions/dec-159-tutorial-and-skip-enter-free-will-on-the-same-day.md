# DEC-159: Played or skipped, free will starts on the same day number

**Ruling.** "same day number for both": the tutorial's 24 hours count as the day before, so a played tutorial and a skip enter free will on the same day.

**Why.** Costs ride the day (DEC-025); a played tutorial must not cost 6.5 % more for the whole run.

**Replaces.** Nothing.
