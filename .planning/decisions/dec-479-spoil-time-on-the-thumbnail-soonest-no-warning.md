# DEC-479: Spoil time on the thumbnail soonest no warning

**Ruling.** "show soonest. just put the time on the thumbnail instead of a bar. we dont have to warn a player who is sleeping that an item will spoil"

**Why.** The thumbnail of a food shows the time until its soonest unit spoils, as text, in place of the freshness bar. The sleep card says nothing about spoiling food.

**Replaces.** DEC-449's "the bar stays" (food only; a cooler's ice bar is not food and is unchanged) and the sleep-card half of FEAT-310. DEC-449 itself is not contradicted: the player still gets a timer.
