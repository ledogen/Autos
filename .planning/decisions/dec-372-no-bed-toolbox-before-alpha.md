# DEC-372: No bed toolbox before the alpha; the roof rack keeps its +4 slots

**Ruling.** "Bail: no bed toolbox; its slots go elsewhere" and "Post alpha, we build a system that allows the Player to
position loads in the bed of the truck. This solves two problems One it lets us put a load behind our toolbox . 2. it
also Automatically forbids cargo that you can't fit. … Pre-alpha, remove the toolbox". The roof rack still adds 4
inventory slots (DEC-129's "4x more inventory slots means +4 slots", carried over unchanged).

**Why.** The toolbox takes the front 0.51 m of a 1.84 m bed; the lumber bundle, plywood, ties and the drum stack do not
fit behind it (card feat204-toolbox-freight). Placing loads in the bed (FEAT-253) is the owner's post-alpha answer.

**Replaces.** DEC-129's toolbox half (the toolbox adds 4 slots). Its roof-rack half stands here. The model
`assets/models/mod-bed-toolbox.glb` stays (ASSET-87 closed when it shipped).

**Source.** Owed card feat204-toolbox-freight, 2026-11-17. Owner's words.
