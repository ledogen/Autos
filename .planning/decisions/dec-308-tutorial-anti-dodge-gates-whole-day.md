# DEC-308: On the tutorial's firing day mom's door stays shut until the errands are done, and only mom's offers sleep

**Ruling.** DEC-282's "both": "mom's door stays shut while the pump is due, and in the evening only mom's offers sleep",
re-stated for DEC-304's order, where the pump and a camp come before the evening. As built: from the card to her sleep,
her door offers nothing until the camping area's beat is over (`_atMomsHouse`), which covers the pump; the camp prompt
shows only at the camping area's stop, and there the camp opens without its sleep, and the truck is never a bed
(`_campPromptState`, `_renderCampUI`). Free will is unchanged (SM-INV-6 holds there).

**Why.** Same as DEC-282: driving home or sleeping early would skip the pump, the camp lesson, the flat, the tow and the
shop. The new order puts a camp inside the day, so the gate now hides sleep rather than the camp.

**Replaces.** DEC-282 (the same two gates, worded for the old evening leg).

**Source.** DEC-282 (owner, card tutorial-gates, 2026-11-16) and DEC-304's order. The owner's words are DEC-282's; the
re-statement is the agent's.
