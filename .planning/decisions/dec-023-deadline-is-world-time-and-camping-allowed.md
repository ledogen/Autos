# DEC-023: A job's deadline is par times its slack, on the world clock

**Ruling.** A job's deadline is par × (1 + slack) measured on world time, and the player may camp with a job in hand; a job still held past its deadline expires and pays nothing.

**Why.** World time means sleeping, fuelling and shopping all spend the same clock the deadline reads.

**Replaces.** Nothing.
