# DEC-101: With 10+ owed items, prefer work that adds nothing to the review pile

**Ruling.** When the owed list holds 10 or more items, prefer work that adds nothing to the owner's review pile (gates, perf, closing on evidence, making a pending decision cheaper to answer); the owner never orders lanes and a lane is never left idle.

**Why.** The owner's review time is the bottleneck.

**Replaces.** Nothing.
