# DEC-035: A worn engine always starts; overheat wears the engine track

**Ruling.** No separate gasket meter: overheat wears the engine track, and a blocked air filter multiplies engine wear up to 20×. A worn engine always starts, at reduced torque.

**Why.** "Otherwise we don't have" a predicament to drive out of — a dead engine at 0 % ends the story instead of making one.

**Replaces.** The never-catch at 0 % (reversed 2026-10-22); a blown-gasket meter (2026-10-16).
