# DEC-345: Katie's picnic at the old McGill ranch drives the snack, the gas lesson and the camp

**Ruling.** Owner, card tutorial-test-drives, 2026-11-16:
"After you drop the pack at Larry's, let's have Katie call you and invite you out to a picnic. That's the impetus for
buying a snack and going to pitch camp. at larry's: "Hey, since I know you got nothing going on today, why don't we go
picnic out at the old McGill ranch. I'll let you pick a spot and I'll meet you out there."
Katie can direct you to the gas station to grab a snack she ends her dialogue with "you know where to go, ill see you
there!"
as you take off the pbrake to leave mom calls "Hey, is everything alright? I almost forgot when i looked over the truck
last night i noticed it was plumb outta gas" and then the gas lesson plays. Since you're already at the gas station,
all you need to do is back out of one spot and park in another. Since Katie's already given you the instructions, once
you fill up with gas and mom sends you on your way, you already have a waypoint to the nearest camping area. The
guiding text can read "drive out to the old McGil ranch". When you arrive at the waypoint, Katie calls you and says,
"Oh you're already there? I'm just a couple minutes out. Go ahead and find a good spot. I'll just pull over where I see
your truck." And she can guide you through the camping menu and the Terrain-based vibe enhancers."

**Why.** The rebuilt tutorial sent the player for a snack and to a camping area with no reason in the story. The picnic
is the reason.

**Replaces.** Nothing by supersession: DEC-304's order stands. How DEC-306, DEC-323 and DEC-324 read alongside it is
recorded by the build (DEC-346..348).

in the note; "McGill" is used.
