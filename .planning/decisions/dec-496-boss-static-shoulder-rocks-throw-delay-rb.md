# DEC-496: The boss slide gets static shoulder rocks, a 3 second delay between throws, and RB throws the charges

**Ruling.** "def too easy to drive around the slides. i think some static rocks on the shoulder would help a lot. make sure they're large enough to appear immoveable so the player doesnt focus explosives on them (a little bigger than the largest rigid body rock we currently have in). i think there should be a painfully slow delay between throws so the player has a sec to aim and really feels the clock: 3 seconds. the rocks read great. the slide does not block the road enough (at least where im manually spawning them). the blast and fuse/impact feel good. rb should throw the charges"

**Why.** The owner's drive of the boss prototype (proto/feat-326-boss @ 74a8e2c3): the truck could skirt a slide on the shoulder, so the charges were not needed.

**Replaces.** Nothing. DEC-491 (about 150 rocks a slide, the 12-point hull) stands.
