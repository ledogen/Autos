# DEC-099: World-gen calls come with before/after screenshots

**Ruling.** A world-gen choice for the owner comes as before/after screenshot sheets with clear choices, never census tables alone, and the metric is derived from the owner's capture.

**Why.** The owner judges a world by looking at it.

**Replaces.** Nothing.
