# DEC-061: Skips are the beta Highway/Shortcut pair

**Ruling.** Fast routes past the network (the Highway/Shortcut pair) are beta, and their tickets are minted at entry.

**Why.** The alpha is one region; a skip has nothing to skip.

**Replaces.** Nothing.
