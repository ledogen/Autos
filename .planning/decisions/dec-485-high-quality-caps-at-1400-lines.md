# DEC-485: The High quality preset renders at most 1400 lines

**Ruling.** "Cap High at 1400 lines (recommended)" (card `perf55-high-res-pick`). The High preset's render height is capped at 1400 lines (Normal caps at 1200). Ultra and every other preset number are unchanged.

**Why.** PERF-55 priced High at device-native resolution; the cap trims that cost.

**Replaces.** High was uncapped (native). Normal 1200, Ultra native stay.

**Source.** Owner, 2026-11-24. Owner's words.
