# DEC-165: The auto shop menu — My Items stays under Shop in the Maintenance tree's design; empty shelf slots stay out

**Ruling.** "3. Update shop design to match what you did on the maintenance tab. 5. yup keep out." My Items (sell,
split / combine, repair inventory parts) stays under the Shop tab, drawn as rows of the Maintenance tree. Empty shelf
slots are left out of the Shop list. Calls 1, 2 and 4 of the same card stand as built: the cold air intake's clean and the
tire-only / wheel-only corner swaps stay hidden until they exist; the transmission keeps its Repair; a cart over budget
blocks checkout, never adding.

**Replaces.** My Items' old panel layout (a selected row with a boxed panel of actions); the "An empty shelf." rows.
