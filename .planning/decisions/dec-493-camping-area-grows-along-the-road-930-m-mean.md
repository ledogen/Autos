# DEC-493: A camping area grows along the road from its seed point to a rolled length, mean 930 m, ±50 %

**Ruling.** "Good: build it out and land". The card it answers, as shown: "an area grows outward along the roads from where the old circle's centre was, by driving distance, until it holds its target length of road. At a junction it carries on down every leg." "the mean target is 930 m of road, which is the median of today's areas. Each area rolls its own target from the seed, uniformly between 465 m and 1395 m (±50 %)." Kept as shown: the map leaves the yellow off a tunnel inside an area (no camping in a bore), and two areas that grow into each other show on the map as one longer area.

**Why.** It is DEC-437 built: an area grown by driving distance is one piece by construction (0 broken areas over 400 random regions, against 29 % today), and it absorbs the junctions it reaches.

**Replaces.** The FEAT-45 disc (radius 350–650 m) as the shape of a camping area, and the size DEC-437 left open. The cell chance (0.26 per 1024 m cell), FEAT-164's 2000 m reach fill and the 40 m road tether are unchanged.
