# DEC-052: The expedition camp is region 1's boss, its own POI type

**Ruling.** The R1 boss is the expedition camp on the wall: a five-line requisition behind the deed gate, its own POI type (the construction site stays a client), with roads trimmed at the wall. The log drag is demoted to a free-roam toy.

**Why.** A boss built from the game's own mission types tests the skills the region taught.

**Replaces.** The bridge site as the first boss (2026-10-12). Settled by FEAT-103 (alpha).
