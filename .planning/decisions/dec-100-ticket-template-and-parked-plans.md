# DEC-100: Tickets use the template; approved plans are parked in the ticket

**Ruling.** Every ticket uses TEMPLATE.md's sections in order (Next steps, Summary, Description, Tracker, Decisions, Open questions, Links, Files, Log); an approved plan is copied into its ticket and committed before a clear.

**Why.** Plan files get overwritten; the ticket is the source of truth.

**Replaces.** Nothing.
