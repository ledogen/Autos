# DEC-438: Hovering the eat icon shows where the energy ends up

**Ruling.** "The hunger preview you get from an item doesn't really show you where the hunger is going to end up because it goes off the scale I think when you're hovering the eat icon it should just show you where the energy will end up just show you the post-eaten energy state"

**Why.** A hunger-recovered box can overrun the scale; the post-meal state always fits.

**Replaces.** Replaces the hunger-recovered preview of FEAT-283. DEC-417 (the button says Eat) stands.
