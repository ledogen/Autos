# DEC-028: Truck upgrades are parts, including the visible mods

**Ruling.** Bed upgrades and bolt-ons (roof rack, toolbox, light bar, bull bar, flatbed, stake bed, long bed) are parts — functions on the truck configuration, never a different vehicle. For the alpha: a 4-reflector incandescent light bar, an in-bed toolbox behind the cab, a roof rack and a bull bar, each doing something.

**Why.** SM-INV-15 holds: an upgrade is not a different vehicle.

**Replaces.** Settled by FEAT-204 (alpha): the roof rack adds inventory slots, replacing items.md's "a second space for long, light loads".
