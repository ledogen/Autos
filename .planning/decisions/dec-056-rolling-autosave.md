# DEC-056: Saving is one rolling autosave slot

**Ruling.** One slot per profile, overwritten about once a second while a run is live, deleted on death. Loading does not delete it.

**Why.** A crash or a closed lid must not cost the run, and one slot can't be save-scummed.

**Replaces.** "Written on quit, deleted on load" (ratified 2026-07-29). Settled by FEAT-42 (alpha).
