the player and the player is so shocked they don't even think about it at the time. Then sometime During day zero mom
asks if you've gotten paid for your last week And realizes that you haven't. And she reminds you on the morning of day
one that you should go pick that up and that's the last portion on rails before you have free will". The owner's "day
zero" is the firing day (day −1 in the code, DEC-159) and "day one" the next morning (day 0); no day is renumbered.
