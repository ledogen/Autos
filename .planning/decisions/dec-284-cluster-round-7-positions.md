# DEC-284: round 7 — key at the corner arc's centre, the dial pair centred between pod and key, tach to 2 o'clock, four lamps in one row

**Ruling.** Two owner notes, verbatim.

Note 1 (the round-5 note in full; round 6 was built from a copy cut off mid-sentence): "keep all sizes. move the key away from the speedo to the corner of the rectangular frame. move the tach right a smidge to give a margin from the temp and gas gauge. then move the speedo right a little bit more so we can open up the tach numbers to about 2 o'clock. move KPH above MPH. then can we make all 4 warning lights the same size and in a straight row on the bottom of the tach? i think it would be cleaner."

Note 2 (on round 6; the newer word wherever they differ): "move the key to the center point of the bottom right frame radius. move the tach and speedo rightward to be centered between the key and the left gauge pod. "

Built as: sizes unchanged; the key's centre is the centre of the bottom-right corner arc; tach and speedo centred between the left pod and the key (equal GAP of housing on each side); the tach's scale ends at 2 o'clock; km/h above MPH; four lamps (brake, tire pressure, check engine, headlamp) the same size in one row along the tach's bottom. Low fuel and overheat stay as dots under E and H.

**Why.** Round 6 look card. Note 2 refines DEC-267 (the key is still in the bottom-right corner, now at the arc's centre), so DEC-267 stands. It replaces DEC-283's positions, which came from the truncated note.

**Replaces.** DEC-283's positions (tach +5, speedo +10, key +10 away). Sizes of DEC-268 stand.

**Source.** Owner, 2026-11-15, round-5 note and round-6 reply, words verbatim.
