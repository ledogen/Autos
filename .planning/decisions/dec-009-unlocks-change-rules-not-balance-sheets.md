# DEC-009: Unlocks widen the deck; they never raise the floor

**Ruling.** Spirits and permanent unlocks change rules, never balance sheets: the sanctioned axis of meta-progression is breadth, not floor. Litmus test: does it raise the floor or make late runs comfortable? If yes, it's illegal however it's dressed.

**Why.** The moment an unlock hands out resources, SM-INV-7 softens into "late runs are comfortable"; this is the invariant most likely to erode one buff at a time.

**Replaces.** Nothing.
