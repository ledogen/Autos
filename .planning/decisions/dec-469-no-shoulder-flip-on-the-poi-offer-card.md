# DEC-469: The shoulder buttons do not flip offers on the POI offer card; its highlightable buttons stay

**Ruling.** Owner, 2026-11-20, after being told that offers at a POI had only ever been flipped by walking the D-pad highlight onto the card's previous / next buttons and pressing A, and that LB / RB (Q / E) flipped them only over the open map: "Okay, yes. Kill the shoulder buttons flipping cards at a POI. Leave the highlightable UI buttons."

Orchestrator's reading, flagged: at a POI with the map closed, LB and RB do not flip offers in either layout, and Q / E do not either. The card's previous and next buttons stay, reachable by the D-pad highlight and A, and by the mouse. Their Q / E / LB / RB keycaps show only while the map is open, where those keys do flip (unchanged). RB on the card keeps its DEC-467 role: the handbrake in Professor, nothing in Ventura Highway. RB / LB flipping the shop's tabs is unchanged.

**Why.** The owner's memory of the shoulders flipping offers was the over-the-map case; at a POI he backs out of the card.

**Replaces.** DEC-468 (LB and RB flip a card's offers when it has more than one).

**Source.** Owner, chat with the orchestrator, 2026-11-20.
