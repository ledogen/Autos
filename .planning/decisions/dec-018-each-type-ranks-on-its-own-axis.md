# DEC-018: Every mission type ranks on its own axis; the tip is speed

**Ruling.** "Rank is the career's core skill. The tip is speed": courier ranks on time, haul on pieces delivered, freight on kg (internal), fragile on remaining health, papers on time against a deadline. Fragile's base is half guaranteed and half × health; destroyed cargo fails the job and pays nothing.

**Why.** "The best way to make missions feel different is to have players focusing on different things in order to unlock the bonus rewards."

**Replaces.** Haul's "no speed tip" (2026-10-16), fragile's condition-only tip (2026-10-14) and "condition does not cut the base" (2026-10-15).
