# DEC-442: No tutorial tip interrupts the actual game; every tip lives in the tutorial

**Ruling.** "The bar up top is the clock on the shop, keep it in the good part and you'll earn the tip. This showed up after my tutorial. It is not acceptable to interrupt gameplay with tutorial tips once you're in the actual game, it all has to be in the tutorial."

**Why.** A tip card after the tutorial interrupts play; the tutorial is the one place that teaches.

**Replaces.** Replaces the first-use cards that fire after the tutorial (the shop-clock tip is the case named). Which first-use cards this covers is FEAT-305's open question.

**Source.** Owner, FEAT-292 card note, answered 2026-11-19. Owner's words.
