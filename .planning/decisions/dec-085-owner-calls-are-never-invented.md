# DEC-085: Owner calls are never invented; a conflict goes to the owner with both sides

**Ruling.** Open design questions are the owner's to answer; when docs or decisions conflict, ask, quoting both sides, rather than picking one.

**Why.** A silently picked side reads as a ruling later.

**Replaces.** Nothing.
