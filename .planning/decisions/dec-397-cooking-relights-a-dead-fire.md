# DEC-397: Cooking at a dead campfire relights it, and the dead fire smokes

**Ruling.** "if you cook at a dead fire the fire is instantly relit. simple, reads nicely." And on the dead fire's look: "add subtle smoke particle effects to the extinguished fire" and "add smoke particle effects to the dead campfire. they should go mostly up, and expand before fading out".

**Why.** The owner's call: simple, and it reads. A burnt-out fire still smoulders, so the ashes give off a thin smoke.

**Replaces.** The hidden cook buttons at a dead fire (FEAT-264's stand-in: buttons hidden and the click refused). DEC-393's note that relighting and cooking at a dead fire were not ruled. DEC-393 stays standing; its "No more flames and smoke particle effects rising from the ashes" now reads as the live fire's look going away, and the dead fire's own thin smoke is this record's.

**Source.** Owner, 2026-11-17, two owed cards: the ASSET-90 dead-campfire look and the FEAT-246 / FEAT-264 camera-campfire look. Owner's words.
