# DEC-430: Demolition signs come from their own pool, visually distinct from road signs

**Ruling.** Owner, in the note box of the owed card `feat278-clutter-look`, 2026-11-18: "Demolition signs should be from a different sign pool. They should still be random, but they need to be visually distinct from road signs. Maybe we make the sign line orange construction signs instead or something."

**Why.** A demolition target must read as a target, not as road furniture.

**Replaces.** Nothing. The orange construction look is the owner's suggestion ("maybe"), not a fixed requirement; the requirement is a separate pool that reads differently from road signs.
