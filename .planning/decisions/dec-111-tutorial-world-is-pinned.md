# DEC-111: The tutorial plays on a pinned or hand-built world

**Ruling.** The tutorial plays on one pinned seed or a hand-built world, named once as the owner's exception to the no-default-seed rule, behind a gate that pins its beats.

**Why.** The tutorial is not a game; its stops need to sit close together.

**Replaces.** "The run's own world" (FEAT-184 choice 2 (owner, 2026-11-07, answered (a))).
