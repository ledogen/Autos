# DEC-060: One generator for everything off the road network

**Ruling.** ONE generator makes spurs, cuts, camping areas, logging sites and POI candidates; the GPS routes by road, and off-network hazards never block a road.

**Why.** One generator means one siting rule to tune.

**Replaces.** FEAT-38 mode B and FEAT-32's siting (absorbed).

**Source.** MILESTONES.md FEAT-52 row; DESIGN.md § The off-network layer ("None of them may ever block the road"). Owner's words.
