# DEC-133: Oil changes happen at auto shops only, and are always available

**Ruling.** An oil change is done at an auto shop only, and every auto shop always offers it. It is the same kind of component as the air filter.

**Why.** One place to do it keeps the service simple to find.

**Replaces.** Nothing.

**Source.** FEAT-202 § Decisions (owner, 2026-11-13). The ticket's wording of the ruling.
