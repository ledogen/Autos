# DEC-005: Wear accrues on time and intensity, never distance

**Ruling.** Wear accrues on time + intensity, never distance: hours and engine torque are both integrated. Easing off the throttle between jobs is how the player protects the truck.

**Why.** It is the lever that splits intense mission driving from casual point-to-point freeroam; fuel is the game's distance cost instead.

**Replaces.** Wear on abuse-events alone (pre-2026-07-19).

**Source.** DESIGN.md § Invariants, SM-INV-5 [RATIFIED 2026-07-19]; § Fuel and gas stations ("Wear prices how hard and how long you drove. Fuel prices how far."). Owner's words.
