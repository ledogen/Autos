# DEC-178: Jobs are hurried or calm; only hurried jobs get the shot clock

**Ruling.** "B: hurried and calm". Courier, fetch, ride and papers are hurried: they get the rank bar. Fragile, haul and freight are calm (no bar) unless the giver is Impatient. Demolition is always calm: the time bar goes and the 40 s hit window is its clock (the dive's demolition question is moot under B).

**Why.** The tip is a clock on every type, but only the types where time is the job should show it; the bar on demolition promised a grade the job does not give.

**Replaces.** The demolition time bar (a bug: its letter is the share smashed); the "no bar on fragile / haul / freight" rule as absolute.
