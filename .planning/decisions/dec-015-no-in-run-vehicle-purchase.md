# DEC-015: You cannot buy a different car during a run

**Ruling.** No in-run vehicle purchase: parts yes, deeply; vehicles no. You do not trade horses; you keep one alive.

**Why.** A new vehicle's price can't be justified against a player barely keeping the current one running, it would dilute the default car, and the game is about maintaining a rig, not acquiring one.

**Replaces.** Nothing.
