# DEC-067: A T always beats no T; departure tests rank, never veto

**Ruling.** A T junction always beats no T: departure tests rank candidates, never veto them; no 45 m guard, and the reuse discount is never rebuilt.

**Why.** Connectivity goes up; a veto silently deletes options.

**Replaces.** The 45 m guard and the reuse discount.
