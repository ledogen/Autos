# DEC-454: The route houses draw calm colours from a bank, per (seed, stop)

**Ruling.** "They're all good to land. My only complaint is the coral color and the teal windows on that house is just a little too eccentric of a color option. Also, whatever happened to using a bank of colors so that even two house models can be recolored to be a different shade?"

**Why.** The recolour pool (`palette` in data/prop-models.js, owner 2026-08-21) is the standing way to keep a world from looking cloned; the three porch houses landed without one, and the ranch's coral walls with turquoise shutters read as too loud.

**Replaces.** Nothing. The ranch's authored default changes from coral/teal to putty tan with brick-brown shutters.

**Source.** Owner, 2026-11-20, on the FEAT-295 / ASSET-91 look card. Owner's words. The five colour sets are the agent's, in the desaturated end of ART-STYLE rule 5.
