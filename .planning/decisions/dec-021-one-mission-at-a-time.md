# DEC-021: One mission at a time

**Ruling.** The player holds one mission at a time; multi-contract bed packing is not part of the game.

**Why.** It keeps each job's load, route and clock legible, and keeps the choice on the board rather than in a bed-packing puzzle.

**Replaces.** Nothing (reaffirmed 2026-10-14).
