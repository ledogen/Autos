# DEC-146: Mom is the true tutorial; Larry reminds you of the camp

**Ruling.** "mom is the true tutorial. she tells you about the camp and good deeds first." Larry then "reminds you about the camp and how to access their missions and beat the game which is an appropriate level of hand holding for a roguelike and not too intrusive".

**Why.** The owner's tutorial dive, question 1 (who sets up the story): mom carries the lessons and the goal, Larry repeats it once.

**Replaces.** DEC-109 (mom sets up the story at the end of the intro; the uncle is no longer the tutorial's voice). Mom now tells the goal first and Larry still repeats it; where the intro ends moved (DEC-147).
