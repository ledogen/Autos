# DEC-439: Item rewards in missions cost 20 percent less, so the item beats the cash

**Ruling.** "Reduce cost of item rewards and missions by 20% If they're full shop price it incentivizes taking the cash because then you have flexibility If there's a little bit of a discounted incentivizes taking the item, which I think we should do"

**Why.** At full price the cash always wins because it is flexible; a discount makes the item the better pick.

**Replaces.** Moves DEC-176 / DEC-254 (the item costs half its shop price) by 20 %. The exact figure (0.50 to 0.40 of shop price, or another reading) is FEAT-303's open question. DEC-176 is not superseded until the number is set.

**Source.** Owner, inbox note 2026-11-19 (08:12). Owner's words.
