# DEC-474: No round POI regions: a driveway, a parking spot, or a shoulder strip at least 2.5 car lengths by 2 car widths

**Ruling.** "We should seek to remove all large diameter circular POI interaction regions. Everything should either be a driveway, a parking spot, or just a rectangular strip on the shoulder. The circle bleeds into the road and is ugly. If we're just marking a spot on the shoulder, make sure it's at least two and a half car lengths and two car widths, so it's easy to just fly in and park."

**Why.** The 10 m orange ring stood round the pad's centre, 12.1 m off the road's centreline, so it reached 2.1 m from the centreline: across the near lane. A spot on the shoulder reads as a place to pull in and never covers the road.

**Replaces.** Nothing recorded. The 10 m interaction radius (`poiInteractR`, owner 2026-08-02, before decision records) and mom's 25 m door radius (`campMomsRadiusM`) are retired by it.

**Source.** Owner, FEAT-318, 2026-11-20, relayed by the orchestrator. Owner's words.
