# DEC-179: An Impatient giver says the job is on the clock

**Ruling.** "the giver's line says it, and the trait line reads 'on the clock'". Impatient givers always speak one of two lines: "Make it quick and I'll throw in a little extra." / "Make sure you head there right away." On fragile, haul and freight the trait line reads "on the clock" (thresholdScale is 1 there, so "rank times 10% tighter" was untrue); courier keeps the tighter-times line.

**Why.** The bar appearing on a normally calm job must be announced by the character, not discovered.

**Replaces.** The trait text "rank times 10% tighter / looser" on non-delivery families.

**Source.** Owner, deep dive QUAL-36-mission-grading, submitted 2026-11-14. Owner's words.
