# DEC-102: Decision records: a record beats DESIGN.md, a ticket and memory, and is frozen

**Ruling.** Every standing ruling is one record; a standing record is frozen except its status line, a change of mind is a new record that supersedes it, and a record beats DESIGN.md, a ticket and memory. Read the governing records before planning a ticket and list every conflict first.

**Why.** Rulings scattered over docs, tickets and memory drift; one frozen record per ruling doesn't.

**Replaces.** Nothing.
