# DEC-282: In the tutorial's evening mom's door stays shut while the pump is due, and only mom's offers sleep

**Ruling.** "both", to the card's two gates: "mom's door stays shut while the pump is due, and in the evening only
mom's offers sleep". As built: while the evening's fill-up is owed her door offers nothing (`_atMomsHouse`), and on
the evening leg the camp prompt never shows, so no camp and no sleep in the truck (`_campPromptState`). Free will is
unchanged (SM-INV-6 holds there).

**Why.** Driving past the pump to mom's and sleeping skipped the pump, the flat, the tow and the shop; sleeping in the
truck or at a camp ended the evening early.

**Replaces.** Nothing; extends DEC-151's traps by two.
