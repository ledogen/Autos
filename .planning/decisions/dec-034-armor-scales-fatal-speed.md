# DEC-034: Armor scales the fatal speed, about 60 to 100 mph

**Ruling.** Armor scales the fatal speed by the hit panel's condition, about 60 mph bare to about 100 mph full, dropping with each hit.

**Why.** It makes armour a real build choice and makes a battered truck honestly more fragile.

**Replaces.** A single fixed fatal speed.

**Source.** DESIGN.md 2026-10-12 pass item (6). Owner's words.
