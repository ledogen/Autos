# DEC-103: Don't open a story milestone whose Requires aren't met

**Ruling.** A story milestone opens only when its `Requires:` are met; free-lane work is always fair game, and tickets are minted at milestone entry, not up front.

**Why.** Prerequisite order keeps half-built systems from stacking.

**Replaces.** Nothing.

**Source.** CLAUDE.md § Story Mode; MILESTONES.md rule. Owner's words.
