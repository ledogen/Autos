# DEC-302: Top parts arrive with the regions and from shops, for now

**Ruling.** "1 and 2 are good for now." Of the card's four ways: (1) region-gated, nothing changes: the reward window (0.7× to 1.3× of a card's top figure) reaches dearer parts as pay grows with the region, and in the alpha they do not drop; (2) purchase: an auto shop stocks the top parts, so cash is always a road to them. No job offers an item above its window (way 3) and none is a deed or expedition reward (way 4).

**Why.** Owner's pick; "for now" leaves ways 3 and 4 open for later.

**Replaces.** Nothing.
