# DEC-342: The bolt-ons' placeholder numbers — prices, shelf odds, armour factor, light gain

**Ruling.** (Agent's wording, awaiting the owner.) The roof rack, bull bar and light bar are parts in their own
sockets, sold on the auto shop's rotating shelf and fitted at the service center like any part:
- Roof rack: $40, 16 kg stowed, +4 slots while fitted (the +4 is DEC-129's).
- Bull bar: $35, 22 kg stowed; while fitted the front panel (`armorFront`) wears 1.5× slower under the same hit.
- Light bar: $25, 8 kg stowed; burns with the headlights (L): the two head beams × 2 intensity and × 1.47 reach.
- Shelf odds: each "Rarely" (weight 1), the lift kit's tier. Fitting labour: rack 1 h, bull bar 1.5 h, light bar 1 h.
- Rewards (FEAT-168's rule: every part is one, under its slot's category, at A–S for an uncommon part): all three in
  `bed`, the bed upgrades' category (FEAT-115), so the work site and the firewood yard hand them out.
- Thumbnails: the rack and bull bar borrow the lift kit's icon, the light bar the headlight's, until their own exist.

**Why.** The owner gave the slots, not the rest. Prices sit by their neighbours: the lift kit $30, the helical LSD
$40, the rucksack $25 (which holds 4 camp items, where the rack holds 4 of anything). The light bar's ×2 is four 55 W
incandescent pods beside two sealed beams; ×1.47 = 2^(1/1.8) is where that gain lights the ground at the bare beam's
end brightness (the head spots' decay is 1.8). The bull bar's 1.5 is the heavy-duty springs' order of lever (×2) cut
back, because front-panel condition also sets the fatal speed (DEC-034).

**Replaces.** Nothing.

**Source.** FEAT-204 wiring agent, 2026-11-16 (land/feat-204-wire). The owner sets the numbers.
