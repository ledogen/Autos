# DEC-198: twilight evenness evens brightness only, never colour

**Ruling.** "3. without contrast it would be boring so no." — the answer to "Evenness evens brightness only, not
colour. Should colour be evened too?" The twilight evenness (`SKY_PARAMS.even`) pulls the sky's brightness toward its
mean and leaves its colour as simulated.

**Why.** Evening the colour too would flatten the sun-side / far-side contrast the owner wants kept.

**Replaces.** The round-4 open question of evening colour as well.
