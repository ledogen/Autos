# DEC-229: A real light at the fuelling area: the bay light, shared

**Ruling.** "we should add a point source light or two to the gas station fueling area"

**Why.** At night the forecourt was lit only by its emissive lenses and signs; nothing cast light on the pumps or a
truck at them.

**Replaces.** Nothing. Built (FEAT-227): the bay light (DEC-228) now serves the fuelling areas too. The one point light
goes to whichever lit place is nearest the truck: the service bay, under the gas station's canopy (3.4 m, over the
island) or over a lone pump island's pumps (3.3 m, under the sign). A second light cost +3.4 % GPU everywhere and was
not shipped (FEAT-227). No shadow (DEC-227).

**Source.** Owner, 2026-11-15, inbox. Owner's words.
