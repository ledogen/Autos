# DEC-425: In story mode rock slides happen only during expedition-camp missions, about three in a whole run

**Ruling.** Owner, in chat, 2026-11-18, on the card `feat26-rockslides-drive`: "it works amazing. def merge. in story mode we only want rockslides during the expedition camp missions. ~3 slides in the whole run. maybe just quietly flag 3 of the exped missions to trigger them or something."

**Why.** The owner's call on pacing: the slide is an event of the run's last act, not a random hazard of every drive.

**Replaces.** For story mode only, DEC-400's "it should only happen once in ~20km driving, but fully random apart from the road section candidacy". DEC-400's other rules stand unchanged (no spirit ties, only where the player can see it coming, a steep enough side slope, the timing aimed at the player, 5–15 rocks, the fade-in), and so does its rate outside story mode. DEC-400 is not superseded as a whole.

**Source.** Owner, in chat, 2026-11-18.
