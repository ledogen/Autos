# DEC-013: Progression gates are diegetic: roads end before the wall

**Ruling.** Every road crossing a region wall is trimmed back to its junction and the region boss opens the way; off-road, a warning then a visible hard wall. In the alpha the road past the expedition camp is blocked by the camp and a barrier, not hidden (FEAT-103).

**Why.** XP-gating harder country needs an in-world frame or it fights the world premise; a softer teleport-and-reorient was rejected as more complicated and less transparent.

**Replaces.** "Region locks are trail-closed barriers a ranger reopens" (struck 2026-10-19). Settled by FEAT-103 (alpha): SM-INV-13's "the network just stops, with no signs" gains the camp's barrier.
