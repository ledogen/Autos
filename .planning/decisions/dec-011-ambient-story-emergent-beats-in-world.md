# DEC-011: The ambient world-story is emergent; authored beats are staged in the world

**Ruling.** The ambient world-story stays emergent (parameter states + the doze) and keyed to run progress; authored story beats are allowed at threshold moments but are staged in real world-space, not an abstract cutscene layer. Characters speak through the chat pane; the world never narrates itself moment to moment.

**Why.** Pushing sleep is how you learn the story; the transgression is the looking.

**Replaces.** The "never scripted events" absolute (relaxed 2026-07-20); metaState as the driver (re-keyed to runState 2026-07-29).
