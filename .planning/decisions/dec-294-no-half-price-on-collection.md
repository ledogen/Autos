# DEC-294: No half price on collection: there is no collection

**Ruling.** "Drop having to come back for the item. The item is paid when the player selects the option in the post mission card immediately and in full Just like any other mission."

**Why.** With no held item there is nothing to collect, so the half-price-on-collection rule has nothing to apply to. The item costs what any job's item costs (DEC-176).

**Replaces.** DEC-252. The mechanic itself is DEC-291; this record exists because a record names one `supersedes`.
