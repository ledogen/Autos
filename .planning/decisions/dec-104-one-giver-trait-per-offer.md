# DEC-104: One giver trait per offer, its effect printed beside it

**Ruling.** One giver trait per offer at most, never stacked, and the card prints the trait's effect in plain numbers on the same line as its name.

**Why.** "I can't tell if the modifiers are actually doing anything."

**Replaces.** "Giver traits, 0–2 per offer." (DESIGN.md line 270, item (7)).
