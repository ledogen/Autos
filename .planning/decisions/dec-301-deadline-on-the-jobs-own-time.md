# DEC-301: A job's deadline runs on the job's own time, and menus cost job time

**Ruling.** "keep menus costing time. the wording is correct. the intention is we want the player to stop and think about their next move and make optimal plays but we dont want to water down mission stress/intensity". The wording approved: "A job's deadline is par times its slack, measured on the job's own time: world time plus any time spent parked on the job." Job time runs under a bare handbrake stop and in every menu that sets the brake (shop, pump, inventory, damage screen, camp card, offer card, map at rest). The player may still camp with a job in hand; a job held past its deadline expires and pays nothing.

**Why.** The world clock holds under the brake so the player can stop and plan; the job's clock does not, so a mission keeps its pressure.

**Replaces.** DEC-023's "measured on world time".
