# DEC-381: The run proper starts on three free meals

**Ruling.** "Let's make sure we get the three free meals when starting a new game."

Reading (the lane's): the three are mom's packed lunches (FEAT-173, items.md §1d: "starts every run with three packed
lunches"). They are granted at the firing, and the tutorial's breakfast card asks the player to eat one, so a played
tutorial reached free will with two or fewer. When the tutorial ends (played or skipped) the packed lunches are topped
back up to three. A run with no tutorial (the paper scenario) starts with its three as before.

**Why.** The tutorial ate into them.

**Replaces.** Nothing.
