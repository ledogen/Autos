# DEC-476: Half of all missions offer an item reward; the gas station hands out springs and shocks

**Ruling.** Owner, on the card `reward-pool-dilution` (FEAT-259), 2026-11-21: "raise the item reward share to about 50%. i.e. half of all missions offer item rewards. add shocks/springs to the gas station pool."

Readings (the lane's): the share is set at the roll that decides whether an offer carries an item (`BONUS_CHANCE` 0.40 → 0.55; 0.40 gave 36.3 of 100 offers an item, 0.55 gives 50.2 over the card's census). "The gas station pool" is read as the gas-station giver's REWARD pool, not its shop shelf (both exist; the card was about rewards). "Shocks/springs" is read as the springs and dampers parts only (stock, heavy-duty, sport), not the anti-roll bars or the lift kit: they get their own reward category, `springs`, which the givers that already had them keep.

**Why.** On the card 63.7 of 100 offers paid cash only and springs plus dampers were 0.7 per 100.

**Replaces.** FEAT-168's [DEFAULT] 0.40 bonus share. DEC-022 (the item is optional, priced off the cash), DEC-226 (cheapest-of-pool fallback), DEC-408 (the tire weights) and DEC-439 (the item discount) stand unchanged.

**Source.** Owner's answer on card reward-pool-dilution, 2026-11-21. Owner's words; readings marked as the lane's.
