# DEC-416: Paper accuracy bad ok great bullseye colours

**Ruling.** "Right now the paper delivery accuracy is shown as \"distance - $x.xx\" . I think instead it should say \"good accuracy - $x.xx\" We should break the accuracies up into bad, ok, great, or bullseye. Blue for bullseye, green for great, yellow for ok, red for bad. Bullseye is within half a meter from the center. For bullseye, instead of it reading \"bullseye accuracy,\" it should just say \"bullseye - $x.xx\""

**Why.** Words and colours tell the player how the throw went; the distance did not.

**Replaces.** Possible overlap with DEC-118 (no letter on result cards) and the paper tip rules (DEC-020, DEC-004); not superseded, owner to confirm.
