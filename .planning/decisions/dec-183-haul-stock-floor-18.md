# DEC-183: The haul stock floor is the 1-deed line, 18

**Ruling.** "raise the stock floor to 18, the 1-deed line". A yard posts 18-30 bundles, so every haul can reach a full deed.

**Why.** A 14-17 yard posted a "Deed: 1 if 18+" line it could not reach.

**Replaces.** The R-06 floor of 14 (the C count).
