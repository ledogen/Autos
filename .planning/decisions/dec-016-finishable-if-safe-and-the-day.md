# DEC-016: The game is finishable if you drive safe; the day in plain words

**Ruling.** "I want the game at a fundamental level to be finishable if you just drive safe": consistent par with no crashes must fund gas, repairs and the upgrades later regions need, while a risky early job may hand large run-layer power. Two average jobs a day driven slowly is fine, one job driven very fast is fine, one job finished slowly is unsustainable, and working fills about 80 % of the day.

**Why.** Any retune of the payout line, the day cost, mission length or day length is checked against these sentences first.

**Replaces.** The pure lazy-day-negative treadmill reading of Open Q4 (a par day merely breaking even).
