# DEC-031: An empty tank or a survivable crash is a predicament; the tow is near-prohibitive

**Ruling.** Running out of fuel or a survivable crash leaves the player in a predicament to solve, not a death. The tow fast-travels to the nearest town but is priced near-prohibitively; a tow the player can't afford ends the run.

**Why.** The predicament is where the game's stories come from; a cheap tow would erase them.

**Replaces.** Nothing.

**Source.** DESIGN.md § Recovery ("priced near-prohibitively"); MILESTONES.md SM-3. Owner's words.
