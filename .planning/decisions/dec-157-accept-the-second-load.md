# DEC-157: A returning player who picks the tutorial gets a second load

**Ruling.** "accept the second load": a later run's intro plays on its own world, and choosing the tutorial at the card loads the tutorial seed.

**Why.** The game holds one world at a time and the intro is staged in it, so it cannot hide the load; first-timers never see it (DEC-149).

**Replaces.** Nothing.
