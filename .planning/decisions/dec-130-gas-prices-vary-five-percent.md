# DEC-130: Gas prices vary ±5 % between stations

**Ruling.** Gas prices differ by ±5 % from station to station. It is mostly eye candy, and prices keep scaling with the run.

**Why.** The variance is flavour, not a trading game.

**Replaces.** Nothing.

**Source.** FEAT-209 § Decisions (owner, 2026-11-13). The ticket's wording of the ruling.
