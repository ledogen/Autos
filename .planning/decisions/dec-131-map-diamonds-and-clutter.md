# DEC-131: Map diamonds are coloured backgrounds; no barrier marks or junction dots

**Ruling.** On the map a diamond is a background that keeps its mission-type colour, split when a place offers several types, and drawn 20 % larger. Barrier indicators and junction dots are not drawn; the legend takes after forest service map branding.

**Why.** One consistent symbol language: a diamond means "work here" and its colour says which kind.

**Replaces.** Barrier indicators and junction dots on the map.
