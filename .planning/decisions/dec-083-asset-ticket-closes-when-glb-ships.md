# DEC-083: An asset ticket closes when the .glb ships; placement is one ticket per role

**Ruling.** An asset ticket closes when its .glb ships; getting it into the world is one consolidated ticket per role (FEAT-71 POI satellites, FEAT-66 camp gear, FEAT-72 road furniture, FEAT-74 POI pools). Never re-open an asset ticket because nothing places it, or re-tag an asset into a pool to make it appear.

**Why.** The tracker should show the one missing system, not a row of finished models held open behind it.

**Replaces.** Nothing.
