# DEC-470: Larry's first meeting explains jobs and the map in about 4 to 6 cards, and does not mention the expedition camp

**Ruling.** Owner, chat with the orchestrator, 2026-11-20, told that Larry's "remember to check out the expedition camp" is the first time anyone names the camp in a played tutorial: "drop larrys mention of the expedition camp its confusing and out of pocket. we want to use every npc interaction to explain the game to spread it out. his story beat should be talking about how everybody needs things and you can find those jobs on the map. mention that there are different mission types, super brief just for player awareness ~4-6 cards. the visit to larrys should be for more than just collecting the pack."

Kept from DEC-361: Larry says the greasebeard line when you drive to his house on the first day. Kept from DEC-322: he tells you to hang onto his old pack.

**Why.** Each NPC meeting should teach one piece of the game, so the explaining is spread out. The camp line pointed back at something the player had not heard.

**Replaces.** DEC-361's "two cards at most" and its expedition-camp clause. The camp is explained by Katie (DEC-347) and by Larry's later call (DEC-326), both unchanged.

**Source.** Owner, chat with the orchestrator, 2026-11-20.
