# DEC-340: Visual mods, round 2 — bent-tube bull bar, cab-footprint rack, lights mount to the rack

**Ruling.** Owner, chat, 2026-11-16, on the first four models ("good first attempt"):
- Bull bar: "the bull bar should be the bent tubular kind so it goes up at an angle. one continuous tube with one
  welded in crossbar" (two reference photos, kept in the model scratch folder).
- Roof rack: "looks a little goofy since its so far back … i dont hate the wire shape and angles but make it fit to
  the footprint of the cab roof." Work on the rack before the lights.
- Lights: "i also dont hate the light pods. if the player has the rack and the lights at the same time the lights
  should mount to the rack. if they only have the lights the lights should get their own bridge member to mount to.
  the lights will sit further forward to clear the rack but might move back to their current location if its not
  there. whartever looks natural."

**Why.** The owner's look at the first renders. A rack hanging past the cab back read wrong; a hoop-and-plate bull bar
is not the part he means.

**Replaces.** Nothing. The bed toolbox drew no comment and stands as built (a crossover box).

**Source.** Owner, chat, 2026-11-16.
