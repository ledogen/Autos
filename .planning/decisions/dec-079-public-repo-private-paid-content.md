# DEC-079: Public repo; paid content never commits here

**Ruling.** The repo is public (CC BY-NC 4.0); paid content (skins, any store item) is served from the owner's server and never committed, and no third-party asset under an NC/ND or other non-commercial licence may enter the repo.

**Why.** An NC asset in the repo would block the owner's own sales.

**Replaces.** Nothing.

**Source.** CLAUDE.md § Constraints (date from git). Owner's words.
