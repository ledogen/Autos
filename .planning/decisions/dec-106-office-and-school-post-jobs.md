# DEC-106: The office and school post their own jobs

**Ruling.** The office and school become givers with the FEAT-153 proposal's jobs (permission slips, science-fair project, signed contracts, payroll envelope); the school still receives class notes from residences only.

**Why.** Both places ended about one board in eight to ten, so a player would very rarely visit them.

**Replaces.** "The office and school post nothing at all, fetch included" (FEAT-89 (alpha; owner, 2026-10-07)).
