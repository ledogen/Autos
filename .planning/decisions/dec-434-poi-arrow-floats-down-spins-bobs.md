# DEC-434: The arrow out to a POI floats above it: points down, spins on y, bobs

**Ruling.** "For wherever relevant, we should turn the arrow that points from the center of the road out to a POI into one that points down and hovers on top of the POI. Like classic video game floating spinning arrow. It should point straight down and rotate on the y-axis.
Turn the arrow that points from the center of the road out to a POI into one that points down from above. Make it rotate on the y-axis and bob slightly up and down. When you're really close to a POI, it's kind of hard to see very high above the building, so make sure the arrow is visible, not Up super high."

**Why.** The sideways arrow from the road reads poorly; a floating marker is the genre's known cue.

**Replaces.** Replaces the FEAT-218 off-road turn arrow (a flat arrow on the road aimed at the objective). Does not touch DEC-303 (start arrow) or DEC-275 / DEC-287.
