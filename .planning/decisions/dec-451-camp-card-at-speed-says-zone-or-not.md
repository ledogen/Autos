# DEC-451: Above the minimum speed the camp indicator says whether you are in a camping zone; only in one does it say to slow down

**Ruling.** "While driving above the minimum speed, the camp indicator should tell you if you're in a camp zone or not. Only once you're in a camping zone should it suggest that you slow down in order to get the details."

**Why.** Telling the player to slow down where there is no camping ground sends them crawling for nothing; the zone is the thing worth knowing at speed.

**Replaces.** The single "slow down to look for a campsite" line shown at speed everywhere (FEAT-45, 2026-10-19 toggle pass). The grading itself still waits for the slow-down (SM-INV-3 note in main.js: no live score at speed).
