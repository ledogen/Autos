# DEC-014: Progress is a count of good deeds, run-layer

**Ruling.** Region access is bought with mission points (good deeds): 1 at rank B or better, ½ at C, 0 at D, read from each type's own (possibly internal) letter, and they reset with the run. Progress must never increase with time taken; every freight job earns at least ½ a deed.

**Why.** A count is legible and impossible to inflate; the half point keeps weak players from stranding.

**Replaces.** XP as the unit (2026-07-29), replaced 2026-08-01; haul share bands and fragile health bands (retired 2026-10-21).
