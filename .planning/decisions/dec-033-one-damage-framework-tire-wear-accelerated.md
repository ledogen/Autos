# DEC-033: One damage framework; tire wear runs faster than real

**Ruling.** Damage and wear share one framework of tracks with severity thresholds; tire wear runs accelerated relative to realism on purpose.

**Why.** Tires are an economic lever, and a lever that never moves in a run teaches nothing.

**Replaces.** Nothing.

**Source.** MILESTONES.md SM-3 ("Twenty-seven tracks in nine classes"); DESIGN.md § Tires ("accelerated relative to realism"). Owner's words.
