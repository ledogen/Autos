# DEC-331: the cluster takes the 2002 owner guide's shape — pods, the cluster outline, a light face

**Ruling.** "Let's reopen the instrument cluster design. I would really love if we could recreate the pods that frame the gauges I think our tachometer and speedo are arranged pretty similar I would like to see the coolant and gas gauge separated slightly left and right and maybe a little bit closer together vertically like the reference and then I would love to see this rectangular background reshaped into the cluster outline so big broad curve on top and flat on the bottom I even think we should add the little inset on the bottom then similarly the coolant and gas gauge should also get a roughly triangular but very automotive curves background as well and then the lighter cluster background behind all of that so two gauges on the left with a dark background in a triangular shape two gauges on the right with pretty much the existing shape of the dark background that we have maybe you can make the bottom flat like the reference and then behind everything is a vaguely cluster shaped light background the key can still stay in the bottom right and we're not adding an oil pressure or battery gauge go ahead and keep the warning lights and shift indicator where they are."

**Why.** The owner's reference is the 2002 Ranger Owner Guide's cluster drawing (artifact XJrmfoRMYu4Rd77onpbAmF).

**Replaces.** Round 8's housing (the arched rectangle) and the upright temp/fuel capsule. DEC-267 (key bottom right), DEC-268 (dial sizes), DEC-284 (GAP between the pods, the lamp row) and DEC-285 (the right edge 37 right of the key, label and lamp sizes) stand; this ruling's words settle the outline's shape.
