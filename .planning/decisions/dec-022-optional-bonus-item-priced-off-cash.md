# DEC-022: A bonus item is a choice the player can take or leave

**Ruling.** A mission's item reward is optional: the player may take the item or its cash value, and an item can be earned at any rank but D. Missions that paid $0 plus an item are retired.

**Why.** "During my playthrough it's just hard to come by money": an item the player doesn't need shouldn't cost them the pay.

**Replaces.** The item-only $0 missions.

**Source.** FEAT-199 source (owner, 2026-11-11 inbox, "we need to make them a choice") and § Verified (2026-11-13). The per-item cash value N is still the owner's number (default: shop price). Agent wording.
