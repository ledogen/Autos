# DEC-396: The road network's existing dead ends stay, as character

**Ruling.** "keep the dead ends as character for now"

**Why.** Not stated. Asked whether main's own dead ends should get the same trim BUG-179 gives the chained fork.

**Replaces.** Nothing. BUG-179 measured 51 dead ends of 32–120 m on 27 of 34 seeds on main: where the other roads at a
junction leave by a T further out, the leftover end of the main road is not trimmed. BUG-179's trim applies only to the
tails its own chained forks would create (the owner did not want NEW dead ends on roads that had none).

**Source.** Chat, 2026-11-17, the owner's answer to the round-2 sheet of BUG-179. Owner's words.
