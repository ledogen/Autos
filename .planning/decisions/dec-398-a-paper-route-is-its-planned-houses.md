# DEC-398: A paper route is its planned houses; the GPS follows the tour through them

**Ruling.** "C. To simplify this, let's still generate 16 targets, but let's only ever show 11, whichever makes the shortest route from Larry's. I think this is already on main, but let's also only ever show the N targets the player is delivering to in the mission. Never any extra distractions" — where C, as put to him, is: only the planned N houses count, and the guide follows the remaining tour. And before it: "I do want the game to direct the player through the shortest straight line distance of N recipients. I also want it to say, 'You missed one,' by pointing the GPS behind them when they drive past a delivery without making it, because it's typically faster to turn around immediately than to get to the end of the route and then turn around."

**Why.** With any of 16 customers counting and the guide going to the nearest one, the line left Larry's toward the cluster, flipped to a lone house the other way, then flipped back: "This is very confusing."

**Replaces.** DEC-371's guide rule (always the nearest house still owed); its turn arrow at each owed house stays. FEAT-141's "any customer counts toward the round's N" (no record of its own).

**Source.** Chat with the orchestrator, 2026-11-17, after driving Larry's first route on seed 3200351065. Owner's words.
