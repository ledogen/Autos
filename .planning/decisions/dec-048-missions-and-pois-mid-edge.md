# DEC-048: Missions and POIs sit mid-edge, never on nodes

**Ruling.** Mission points and POIs sit mid-edge along roads, never on junction nodes.

**Why.** A junction is already a decision point; a POI on it crowds the geometry and the choice.

**Replaces.** Nothing.
