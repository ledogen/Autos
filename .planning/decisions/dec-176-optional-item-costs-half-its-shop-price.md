# DEC-176: Taking an optional item costs half its shop price; the item is priced about the job's pay

**Ruling.** Taking a mission's optional item costs half its shop price out of the job's cash. The item a job offers has a shop price about equal to the job's pay, and may be up to 30 % over it.

**Why.** "This way you cant sell it for more money you have to want the item to take it." "The reward item price can be like 30% over the mission pay but usually is about equivalent."

**Replaces.** The built default cost of the full shop price (ITEM_COST_FRAC 1), and the cap's role as the rule for which items may ride a job.
