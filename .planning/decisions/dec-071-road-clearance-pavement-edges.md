# DEC-071: Adjacent roads keep 12 m of daylight between their pavement edges

**Ruling.** Adjacent roads keep 12 m of daylight between their pavement edges, and neither road's earthwork may reach the other; a near-miss of under ~1 m over under ~20 m between legs of one node is tolerated.

**Why.** "Make the secondary edge aware of the primary and give it a berth."

**Replaces.** Settled by FEAT-77 (alpha): memory's "12 m centre-to-centre".

**Source.** FEAT-77 § Owner rulings 2026-09-07/08 and § RULED 2026-11-01 (a). Owner's words.
