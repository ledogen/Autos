# DEC-238: The tutorial's morning runs mom's errand: Larry's old pack

**Ruling.** "lets have mom send you on a an errand to larry's - return his old pack. mom tosses the pack out her front door and it lands on the ground by the steps. then we give the lesson that you can press a button to load/collect loose nearby cargo. then you go to larry's. out of goodwill he decides to give you his old camping pack, complete with bedroll."

**Why.** The tutorial's 24 h want real lessons rather than a clock jump; this one teaches the pick-up and hands the player the camping kit through the story.

**Replaces.** The morning's straight drive from mom's to the burger joint (DEC-153's last act stays: the paycheck at the joint). As built, the starting kit's pack (with its bedroll) is that pack: a played tutorial takes it off the truck at "play", and a skip puts it back.
