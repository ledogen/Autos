# DEC-424: The camp keeps its crossing unless the requisition is over 15 % off a 22 km target; then it takes the crossing nearest 22 km. No pumps as fuel sources

**Ruling.** Owner, in chat, 2026-11-18, to three questions put by the orchestrator: "1. A  2. 22  3. no". Question 1 was how often the camp may move: A = "Move only if over 15 % off target" (B was "Always take the crossing nearest the target"). Question 2 was the target for the requisition's loaded total: 22 km (the other choice was 25.9 km, today's median). Question 3 was whether the fuel line may start at any gas pump: no. The owner's reason for the whole change, from the same day: "I feel like the requisitions were incredibly long … right now it seems the player is at the mercy of world generation, and that it can just be that hard … What can we do to even out requisition length between seeds?" Offered with the three questions and not objected to: each line leaves its source by the nearer end of the source's road, not by a seeded coin flip.

**Why.** Every region holds exactly one source of each type, so choosing sources cannot even anything; the camp's crossing is the only lever (5 to 17 per region). Measured on 61 seeds: today 18.0–34.8 km (hardest ÷ easiest 1.93); this rule at 22 km 17.5–26.9 km (1.54), 37 of 61 camps move, the owner's lost run's seed 660454599 goes 33.1 → 23.6 km.

**Replaces.** The camp siting's "farthest qualifying crossing from spawn" as the only preference (FEAT-103); the planner's seeded choice of which end of the source's road a requisition line leaves by.

**Source.** Owner, in chat, 2026-11-18; card `req-evening-options`; study files in CarGame-land-feat180-dusk/wip/req-length/.
